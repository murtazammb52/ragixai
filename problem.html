The Root Cause

Why Traditional Search Fails

Keyword-based search was built for a different era. It simply cannot handle the density and complexity of SEC financial filings.

🔤

Dense Financial Language

Analysts ask questions in plain English but 10-K filings use complex legal and accounting language. "What are the revenue drivers?" won't match "net revenue attributable to product segment growth."

🧩

Cross-Filing Context

A question about a company's risk exposure spans dozens of sections across multiple filings. Traditional search returns a list of document links — not a synthesized, cited answer.

📎

No Source Traceability

Financial professionals must cite specific filing sections and page numbers. Generic search provides no link between the answer and the exact EDGAR document section that supports it.

🌫️

AI Hallucinations

Basic LLM chatbots without retrieval confidently fabricate financial figures. In investment, compliance, or legal contexts this is catastrophic — decisions made on wrong numbers.

Financial Reality

The Real Pain Points

Specific, measurable problems analysts and investors face when working with SEC filings every day

📄

Overwhelming Filing Volume

A single 10-K can exceed 300 pages. Analysts covering 10+ companies must manually read thousands of pages per quarter — an impossible task at scale.

🔍

No Semantic Search on EDGAR

EDGAR's full-text search is keyword-only. Searching for "supply chain risk" won't find filings that discuss "logistics disruptions" — missing critical disclosures.

📊

Cross-Filing Comparison

Comparing risk factors or revenue trends across multiple companies requires downloading and manually reading dozens of filings. There is no unified query interface.

⚖️

Compliance & Audit Risk

Compliance teams must cite the exact filing section and page for every regulatory finding. Manual search makes this slow and error-prone — a serious legal liability.

💰

Investment Due Diligence

Investors running due diligence need answers fast. "What are this company's major risk factors compared to last year?" can take hours — RAGixAI answers in seconds.

🤖

Hallucination in Finance

Generic LLMs invent financial figures. A fabricated revenue number or debt ratio cited in an investment memo is not just wrong — it's potentially illegal.
